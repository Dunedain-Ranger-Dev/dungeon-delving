# Dungeon factions

Each run is held by one faction: its spawners spawn only that faction's mobs, and its boss is one of the faction's
bosses. Mobs get stronger by tier through tougher **variants**, not stat boosts.

Designed by the user, 2026-10-22.

## Undead (MVP)

Replaces the old skeleton faction. The MVP's only faction.

| Tier | Mobs | Boss |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Skeleton, Skeleton Swordsman, Zombie | Zombie Lord |
| 2 | Stray, Stray Swordsman, Husk | Skeleton Lord (a Stray Swordsman in full armor) |
| 3 | Bogged, Bogged Swordsman, Drowned | Barrow King |
| 4 | Wither Skeleton, Wither Skeleton Archer, Wither Skeleton Mage (thematic spells) | Lich Lord (looks like a husk, moves at skeleton speed, casts spells) |

## Goblins (Phase 2)

- **All tiers:** Goblin Warriors and Goblin Archers. Weak, no armor; their danger is numbers, with larger packs at
  higher tiers.
- **T2–4:** Goblin Shamans (poison and healing) and Goblin Pyros (pure fire magic). No armor, except maybe something
  like a goblin mask.
- **T3–4:** Hobgoblin Soldiers (melee, shields, medium-style armor) and Hobgoblin Hunters (crossbows). Hobgoblins are
  full-sized (goblins are short); illagers can be the basis.
- **T4:** Hobgoblin Spellbreakers (dual-wield, light armor, target spellcasters first) and Hobgoblin Vanguards (heavy
  armor, sword and board).
- **Bosses:** T1 Goblin Gladiator (a big goblin that dual-wields), T2 Goblin Shaman (spellcaster), T3 Hobgoblin
  Veteran (a stronger Vanguard), T4 Hobgoblin General (ideally switches between ranged and melee).
